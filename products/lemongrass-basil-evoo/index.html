Infused with the essence of Thai and Asian culinary traditions, our Lemongrass Basil blend marries the tangy zest of lemongrass with the aromatic verdancy of basil. This duo imparts a uniquely refreshing and herbaceous twist to dishes like pork, white fish, and chicken, infusing them with an alluring taste of the exotic. Transform your salads with a dressing that’s as flavorful as it is innovative, or use it as a superb marinade to elevate your cooking. For a snack that’s both nutritious and enjoyable, try tossing chickpeas with Lemongrass Basil and roasting them briefly — a simple yet sublime treat. Give it a try, and it’s sure to become a new favorite. Bon appétit! The High Phenolic Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO) featured in each of our infused concoctions is a powerhouse of micronutrients and polyphenols, offering a spectrum of health benefits. These include antioxidant effects, anti-inflammatory properties, heart and brain protection, cancer risk reduction, diabetes management, and antimicrobial activity. It’s an abundant source of vitamins E and K and maintains a favorable balance of Omega 6 to Omega 3 fatty acids. The phenolic compounds within also play a vital role in gut health, curbing harmful bacteria, fostering beneficial ones, and boosting the generation of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) by gut microbes.
