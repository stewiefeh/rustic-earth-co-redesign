Garlic enthusiasts, rejoice! Originating from Central Asia and the Mediterranean region, garlic often leaves people pondering: Is it a herb, a spice, or a vegetable? In truth, garlic belongs to the lily family, which also encompasses onions, shallots, and leeks. Renowned for its robust scent and bold flavor, garlic undergoes a transformation when roasted or sautéed, becoming sweet and rich. A cornerstone of global culinary traditions, garlic is virtually irreplaceable in cooking, much like onions. Elevate your culinary creations with this subtly complex and richly flavored olive oil, perfect for enhancing meats and vegetables, enriching salads, accompanying bread, or adding a finishing touch to pizza crust before it hits the oven. The High Phenolic Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO) featured in each of our infused concoctions is a powerhouse of micronutrients and polyphenols, offering a spectrum of health benefits. These include antioxidant effects, anti-inflammatory properties, heart and brain protection, cancer risk reduction, diabetes management, and antimicrobial activity. It’s an abundant source of vitamins E and K and maintains a favorable balance of Omega 6 to Omega 3 fatty acids. The phenolic compounds within also play a vital role in gut health, curbing harmful bacteria, fostering beneficial ones, and boosting the generation of short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) by gut microbes.
